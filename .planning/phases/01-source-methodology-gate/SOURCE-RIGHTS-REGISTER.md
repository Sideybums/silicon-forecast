# Source Rights Register — Phase 1 Candidate Routes

**Register date:** 2026-08-05
**Intended Phase 1 use:** scheduled collection for a private commercial 32GB DDR5 desktop-kit regional index; permitted raw/evidence retention; permanent derived history; private display. Public/commercial display is assessed separately and remains locked.
**Decision boundary:** rights diligence, not legal advice. Technical access and vendor availability are not permission. No account was created, contract accepted, payment made or production data collected.
**Approval state:** every row is `production_approval: not_approved`. No route in this register is an approved production source.

## Register controls

- Statuses are exactly `verified_permitted`, `verified_restricted`, `contract_required`, `unknown` and `not_applicable`, as defined in `SOURCE-RIGHTS-TEMPLATE.md`.
- Source IDs are stable. IDs already present in the UK/Germany report (`UK-1`…`UK-5`, `DE-1`…`DE-6`) are preserved. Stable IDs were assigned to the US and Japan report rows in their report order (`US-1`…`US-8`, `JP-1`…`JP-7`).
- Report shorthand such as “VP after CR” and “U-CR” is normalised without claiming approval: an unmet account/programme/contract condition is `contract_required`; silence or inaccessible controlling terms is `unknown`; an express conflict is `verified_restricted`.
- All records are version `1`, owner `source-rights owner`, change request `initial-draft`, created/reviewed `2026-08-05`, next review due `2026-09-04`, and fail closed if evidence expires or the route/terms change. First-party web evidence has a maximum 90-day life under the template; sales/enquiry evidence has a 30-day life. The earliest applicable evidence expiry is therefore `2026-09-04` unless refreshed sooner.
- `diligence_only` authorises written enquiry/contract review only. It does not authorise collection, account creation, spend or use. `blocked` means the present route conflicts with or cannot evidence Phase 1 use.

### Common written questions

1. **Q1 — purpose and automation:** Does the controlling licence expressly permit scheduled collection/delivery for commercial market-index construction, not merely affiliate traffic, repricing, seller operations or internal category management?
2. **Q2 — raw/evidence retention:** May raw observations and policy-compliant evidence records be retained indefinitely for audit, including after termination? What must be deleted?
3. **Q3 — derived history:** May non-reconstructable indices be created, owned, retained permanently, combined with competing sources and survive termination?
4. **Q4 — display:** What private, paying-customer and public display modes are allowed, with what aggregation, delay, attribution, timestamps, logos, links and click tracking?
5. **Q5 — redistribution/processors:** Is raw redistribution prohibited, and are tightly controlled processors, auditors and regulators allowed access?
6. **Q6 — upstream rights:** Identify all retailers/upstream sources and collection methods; warrant and indemnify the licensed use, including database/IP rights.
7. **Q7 — identifiers:** Supply the dictionary and DDR5 completeness/provenance for MPN, GTIN/UPC/EAN/JAN, platform SKU, capacity, module count, speed, timing, ECC/buffering, seller and condition.
8. **Q8 — tax and discounts:** Define tax/VAT/consumption-tax inclusion and treatment of automatic discounts, coupons, member prices, rebates and points.
9. **Q9 — delivery:** Provide numeric mandatory delivery to the fixed regional destination, or state that landed price cannot be derived; define free thresholds, pickup and membership assumptions.
10. **Q10 — stock and time:** Define orderability, stock/back-order/pre-order, observation timestamp/time zone, stale removal and source refresh.
11. **Q11 — operations:** Provide SLA, rate limits, schema/version/deprecation notice, corrections/restatements, historical reproducibility and source/panel churn notice.
12. **Q12 — panel and independence:** List current DDR5 retailers/panel members, source overlap, seller/marketplace treatment, paid-placement bias, weighting and inclusion/removal history.

No source clears the private Phase 1 gate of `verified_permitted` for AC, RR, DH, PD and CSC with current, applicable evidence.
