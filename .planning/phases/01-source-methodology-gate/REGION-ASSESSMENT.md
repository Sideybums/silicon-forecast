## Why breadth is not source independence

A comparison engine or extraction vendor can expose hundreds or thousands of shops while still being one source for resilience and rights analysis. Retailer rows delivered by the same vendor can share:

- one contract and termination event;
- one crawler, matching model, schema, refresh schedule and outage domain;
- the same upstream comparison engine or merchant feed;
- duplicated offers from the same retailer, marketplace seller or affiliate feed;
- correlated ranking, paid-placement, coverage and correction biases.

Therefore shop count is not source count. PriceRunner plus PriceAPI's PriceRunner route is not two independent sources. PriceAPI routes to idealo, billiger or marketplaces must be traced to their upstream offer IDs and licences before any independence claim. Multiple Awin advertisers diversify retailers but still share an affiliate-network delivery and programme-risk layer; each advertiser's terms must be separately evidenced.

For the Phase 1 portfolio, a countable source must have a stable identity, a governing rights record, a distinct upstream observation path, source-level timestamps and offer IDs, and a documented failure/overlap domain. Duplicate retailer-product-offer observations are de-duplicated before measuring breadth.

### Route to three sources

The UK diligence hypothesis is:

1. **PriceRunner UK commercial API** as one multi-shop comparison source, if an executed data schedule clears the mandatory rights and semantics.
2. **One or more named Awin DDR5 advertiser feeds**, after each advertiser grants programme/feed access and captured terms reveal no explicit conflicting restriction; the network itself is not automatically a second source for every feed.
3. **A separately licensed direct retailer or marketplace route** with a distinct contract and acquisition path. eBay may be examined only after its current developer agreement is obtained; Amazon is excluded under standard terms for historical indexing.

PriceAPI is valuable for technical and contractual comparison, but it cannot be counted as independent where it merely mediates PriceRunner, idealo, Amazon, eBay or another source already in the portfolio.

If three sufficiently independent sources cannot be evidenced, the only alternative is an **explicit reduced-source experimental designation**. That designation must be a separate human decision recording the exact source count, concentration, permitted private audience, duration, quality label, publication lock, exit criteria and revocation conditions. It does not make a region production-ready, does not clear a source's rights and must not be presented as a supported regional index.

## Concrete acceptance criteria

These are proposed decision thresholds for future diligence; they are not claims that any candidate meets them.

### A. Source-level pass criteria

A candidate can be put forward for human source authorisation only if all of the following are evidenced by applicable terms, the approved source-use policy, access state and data dictionary:

1. Automated collection/delivery at the proposed cadence is authorised and technically available, with no explicit restriction conflicting with index construction.
2. No applicable term explicitly conflicts with immutable retention of the minimal factual evidence payload.
3. No applicable term explicitly conflicts with permanent derived daily prices and indices, including post-termination history.
4. The intended private/public use has no explicit conflict; public/paying-customer display remains separately human-locked.
5. Upstream source identity is sufficient to assess overlap and database/IP risk; required attribution and audit obligations are executable.
6. No term requires price content to be deleted after 24/72 hours, remain untransformed, serve only affiliate click generation, or be withheld from private analysis.
7. Every included observation has a stable source/offer ID, source timestamp and time zone, retailer/seller, condition, currency, price, tax treatment, delivery treatment and stock/orderability state. Missing mandatory fields fail closed.
8. Every included listing matches by exact manufacturer MPN, validated GTIN/EAN or a human-reviewed mapping. Fuzzy-only matches are excluded.
9. For a fixed UK postcode, VAT and mandatory delivery can be calculated deterministically in GBP, or the offer is excluded. No silent imputation or currency conversion is allowed in the first UK index.
10. The contract states refresh cadence, correction/restatement process, schema/version notice, rate limits and service expectations; a sample can be replayed to the same source observations and totals.

Any failure is a **source-level fail** for the proposed use. A restriction on raw redistribution is acceptable for the private MVP; it must be recorded and enforced. Unclear public-display rights keep public display at no-go without necessarily defeating a fully cleared private-only route.

### B. Portfolio-level pass criteria

The UK can be put forward for human production-region approval only if either:

- **Standard route:** at least three countable, sufficiently independent sources meet all private-MVP source-level criteria; no common source or acquisition vendor supplies more than 50% of qualifying retailer-day observations in a representative sample; upstream overlap is measured and de-duplicated; or
- **Experimental route:** an authorised human signs a reduced-source experimental designation with an explicit source count, concentration limit, private-only scope, visible experimental label, expiry date and publication lock.

For a standard-route data sample, require all of the following before the region decision is reconsidered:

- at least 20 eligible 32GB DDR5 desktop-kit canonical products from at least four manufacturers after exact-match and eligibility exclusions;
- qualifying observations from each countable source on at least 90% of 14 consecutive authorised test days;
- at least 95% successful scheduled deliveries per source during that test, with every failed or stale delivery visible;
- 100% deterministic exclusion of observations with unknown tax, delivery, currency, condition, stock or provenance;
- identical recalculation from the fixed preserved input set and calculation version.

The 14-day diligence sample does not replace the later 30-day private shadow-run requirement.

## Stop and pivot rule

### Immediate stop for a route

Stop diligence on a candidate for this use if the vendor:

- refuses to provide the controlling agreement or answers only with marketing material;
- has an explicit retention, derivation, deletion, unchanged-reproduction or affiliate-only restriction that conflicts with index construction;
- will not identify the upstream source and contractual basis sufficiently to assess rights and overlap;
- cannot provide deterministic tax, delivery, currency, stock, timestamp and identifier semantics; or
- requires collection before applicable terms and explicit restrictions can be reviewed.

### UK continuation test

After written responses from PriceRunner UK and PriceAPI UK are reviewed, continue UK diligence only if:

1. at least one route meets every source-level access, explicit-restriction and data-quality criterion; and
2. documentary evidence shows a credible path to two additional independent sources, or a human is willing to consider the defined experimental route.

If either condition fails, stop UK production planning and pivot diligence to Germany. Do not keep the UK merely because its score is close to Germany's.

### Germany and overall stop

For Germany, stop if solute will not supersede its retention/display restrictions and neither PriceAPI nor PriceRunner supplies a territory-specific, rights-complete alternative with a credible independent portfolio path. If both UK and Germany hit their stop conditions, keep Phase 1 at **NO-GO**: do not build production ingestion. The next choice is either a separately authorised experimental designation, a new licensed-source research round, or a product/methodology re-scope. Japan's BCN transaction-price model must not be substituted silently for a retail-offer index.
