## Product direction clarification — 2026-08-09T16:31Z

David clarified that the product's central question is not merely “how have prices moved since collection began?” It is “how expensive are components relative to a defensible recent-history affordability/reference period, and are they moving back towards that range?” The motivating context includes AI-era hardware demand and shortages alongside trade, war, logistics, energy and macroeconomic shocks; those events remain explanatory overlays and never numeric inputs.

The architecture now separates:

1. Native basket-vintage arithmetic scales.
2. A basket-vintage-linked nominal category history.
3. A separately evidence-approved historical-reference presentation at 100.
4. A monthly constant-price series using a specifically approved official UK deflator and pinned release-vintage lineage.
5. An optional, separate earnings-relative affordability measure.
6. Event overlays whose creation or revision cannot change any numeric checksum.

The private 2026-08-09 diagnostic remains useful for prospective collection, but its inception value of 100 is not normal, affordable or the supported product baseline.
